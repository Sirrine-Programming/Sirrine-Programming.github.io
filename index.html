Engineering Standard

The Sirrine Programming Pillars

Every title is engineered against strict comfort, hardware performance, and intuitive spatial interaction standards.

First-Principles Physics & Immersion

We eschew canned animations in favor of physical simulation—coupled pendulums in paragliders, atmospheric drag models, and inertial 6DOF thrusters that players feel in their hands.

Rock-Solid Frame Timing & Comfort

Designed from day one for 72/90/120 Hz lockstep on Meta Quest 3 and 3S hardware. GPU-instanced draw calls, single-pass stereo multiview, and comfort reference frames reduce nausea and fatigue.

Voice & Natural Spatial Interaction

Spatial computing is greatest when unburdened by flat menus. We pioneer conversational agent pipelines, unholstered motion tracking, and physical room passthrough interactions.
